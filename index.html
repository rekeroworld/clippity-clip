clippity clip

clippity clip is a personal tool that posts short gaming video clips, which the owner has permission to share, to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. Every clip is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is posted, and captions credit the original creator.

It serves a single account and does not post for other users.
