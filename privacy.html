Clippity Clip - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Clippity Clip ("the App", "we", "us") handles. The App is a personal publishing tool that uploads the owner's approved gaming highlight videos to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

1. What we collect

When you authorize the App through TikTok's official login, TikTok gives the App:

  • An access token and a refresh token, used only to post to your account.
  • Your TikTok open ID, which identifies your account to the App.
  • Basic profile information that TikTok shares under the permissions you approve, such as your display name.

The App also handles the videos and captions you choose to upload.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your TikTok password. Login happens on TikTok's own pages.
  • We do not collect payment information.
  • We do not read your private messages, followers list or watch history.
  • We do not run ads, tracking pixels or analytics on viewers.

3. How we use it

We use this data only to:

  • Keep you signed in so the App can post.
  • Upload the videos and captions you have approved to your own TikTok account.
  • Show you the status of an upload.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Where it is stored

Tokens, videos and captions are stored on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server other than TikTok's, as part of posting. Tokens are not shown in logs or shared.

5. How long we keep it

We keep tokens until you revoke access or we delete them. Videos and captions stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions.
  • Ask us to delete any data we hold about you by contacting us below.

7. Third parties

Posting goes through TikTok's platform. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs data TikTok holds. We do not control it.

8. Children

The App is not for anyone under the minimum age TikTok requires to hold an account, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

We keep tokens on the owner's own machine and do not share them. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
